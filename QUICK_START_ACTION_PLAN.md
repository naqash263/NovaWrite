## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Send generic outreach emails
- Spam communities with links
- Ignore personalization
- Give up after one follow-up
- Focus only on quantity over quality

✅ **Do:**
- Personalize every email
- Provide value before asking
- Build relationships
- Follow up strategically
- Track everything
