## 🎯 Content Strategy Overview

### **Publishing Schedule**
- **Frequency**: 2 posts daily
- **Morning Post (8 AM)**: Technical tutorials, how-to guides, tool reviews
- **Evening Post (6 PM)**: Industry insights, trends, quick tips, case studies
- **Word Count**: 1000-2000 words per post
- **Target**: 60+ posts per month

### **Content Pillars**
1. **AI Automation** (40% of content)
2. **Business Intelligence** (30% of content)
3. **CRM Integration** (20% of content)
4. **Industry Insights** (10% of content)

## 📝 Editorial Guidelines

### **Content Quality Standards**

#### **✅ Must-Have Elements**
- [ ] Compelling headline with primary keyword
- [ ] Clear introduction with problem statement
- [ ] 3-5 main sections with H2 headings
- [ ] Practical examples and screenshots
- [ ] Actionable takeaways
- [ ] Internal links (3-5 per post)
- [ ] Call-to-action at the end
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Featured image with alt text

#### **❌ Content to Avoid**
- Generic or recycled content
- Posts without practical value
- Content without proper SEO optimization
- Posts without clear structure
- Content that doesn't align with expertise
- Posts without proper research

### **Writing Style Guidelines**

#### **Tone & Voice**
- **Professional yet approachable**
- **Technical accuracy with simplicity**
- **Confident and authoritative**
- **Helpful and solution-oriented**

#### **Language Rules**
- Use active voice (80% of the time)
- Write in second person ("you", "your")
- Use short sentences (15-20 words average)
- Include bullet points and numbered lists
- Use subheadings every 200-300 words
- Include relevant examples and case studies

## 🔍 SEO Optimization Rules

### **Keyword Strategy**

#### **Primary Keywords (1 per post)**
- Include in title, H1, first paragraph
- Use 2-3 times naturally in content
- Include in meta description

#### **Secondary Keywords (3-5 per post)**
- Use in H2 headings
- Include 1-2 times in content
- Use in image alt text

#### **Long-tail Keywords (5-10 per post)**
- Include naturally throughout content
- Use in internal links
- Include in conclusion

### **Content Structure Rules**

#### **Headlines**
- **Format:** [How to/What/Why] + [Action/Topic] + [Tool/Year]
- **Length:** 50-60 characters
- **Include:** Primary keyword
- **Examples:**
  - "How to Build Your First n8n Workflow in 2024"
  - "Power BI vs Tableau: Complete Comparison Guide"
  - "5 AI Automation Tools Every Business Needs"

#### **Meta Descriptions**
- **Length:** 150-160 characters
- **Include:** Primary keyword + benefit
- **Format:** [What] + [How] + [Why it matters]
- **Example:** "Learn how to build AI automation workflows with n8n. Step-by-step tutorial with screenshots. Boost your productivity in 30 minutes."

#### **Internal Linking Rules**
- **Minimum:** 3 internal links per post
- **Maximum:** 5 internal links per post
- **Anchor text:** Use descriptive, keyword-rich text
- **Link to:** Related tutorials, tool reviews, case studies

### **Image Optimization**

#### **Requirements**
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for screenshots
- **Size:** 1200x630px for featured images
- **Alt text:** Descriptive, include primary keyword
- **File name:** Descriptive, use hyphens

#### **Alt Text Examples**
- ❌ "screenshot"
- ✅ "n8n workflow builder interface showing automation nodes"
- ❌ "chart"
- ✅ "Power BI sales dashboard with revenue analytics"

## 🚀 Getting Started

### **Week 1: Setup**
1. **Create content calendar** for next 4 weeks
2. **Set up tracking tools** (Analytics, Search Console)
3. **Create content templates** and style guide
4. **Research 50+ blog topics** from the provided list
5. **Plan first week's content** in detail

### **Week 2: Launch**
1. **Start with 1 post daily** to establish routine
2. **Follow templates** exactly for consistency
3. **Monitor performance** daily
4. **Adjust strategy** based on initial results
5. **Scale to 2 posts daily** after 1 week

### **Week 3-4: Optimization**
1. **Analyze performance** of first 2 weeks
2. **Identify top-performing content** types
3. **Adjust content mix** based on data
4. **Improve underperforming posts**
5. **Plan next month's content**
