## Content Quality Standards

### SEO Optimization
- **Target Keywords**: Each post targets 3-5 relevant keywords
- **Meta Descriptions**: 150-160 characters, compelling and descriptive
- **Title Tags**: 50-60 characters, keyword-rich and click-worthy
- **Header Structure**: Proper H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
- **Internal Linking**: Link to other relevant posts and pages
- **External Linking**: Link to authoritative sources

### Content Structure
- **Introduction**: Hook the reader, state the problem/solution
- **Table of Contents**: For posts over 1,500 words
- **Main Content**: Well-structured with subheadings
- **Code Examples**: Where applicable, with syntax highlighting
- **Screenshots/Images**: Visual aids for better understanding
- **Conclusion**: Summary and call-to-action
- **Related Posts**: Suggest 3-5 related articles

### Engagement Elements
- **Call-to-Actions**: Encourage comments, sharing, and engagement
- **Questions**: End with questions to encourage comments
- **Social Sharing**: Easy sharing buttons
- **Newsletter Signup**: Promote email subscription
- **Resource Downloads**: Link to free tools and templates

## Technical Implementation

### Blog Post Template Structure
```markdown
# [Title] - [Target Keyword]

## Introduction
- Problem statement
- Solution overview
- What readers will learn

## Table of Contents
- [Link to section 1]
- [Link to section 2]
- etc.

## [Main Content Sections]
- Detailed explanations
- Code examples
- Screenshots
- Step-by-step instructions

## Conclusion
- Key takeaways
- Next steps
- Call-to-action

## Related Resources
- [Link to related post 1]
- [Link to related post 2]
- [Link to free resource]
```
