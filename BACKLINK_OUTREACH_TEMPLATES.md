## Best Practices for Outreach

### Email Personalization
- ✅ Always use the recipient's name
- ✅ Mention specific content from their site
- ✅ Reference their recent articles/posts
- ✅ Show you've done research

### Subject Lines
- ✅ Keep under 50 characters
- ✅ Be specific, not generic
- ✅ Include value proposition
- ❌ Avoid spam words

### Timing
- ✅ Send Tuesday-Thursday
- ✅ Best times: 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM (recipient's timezone)
- ✅ Follow up after 5-7 days if no response

### Tracking
- ✅ Use email tracking tools (Mailtrack, HubSpot)
- ✅ Track open rates
- ✅ Track response rates
- ✅ A/B test subject lines
